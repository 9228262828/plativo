PLATIVO PRIVACY

Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 16, 2026

Summary: PLATIVO is an offline-first weekly meal planner. The current core version does not require an account, Firebase, backend, cloud sync, ads, analytics, location, camera, microphone, contacts, or payment information.

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how PLATIVO, package com.plativo.mealplanner, handles information in the current core version of the app.

2. Scope

This Policy applies to the PLATIVO mobile application and these informational support pages.

3. Offline-First Design

PLATIVO is designed as an offline-first weekly meal planning and meal rotation application. Its current core features do not require an internet connection.

4. No Account Required

You do not need to create a PLATIVO account, username, password, or personal profile to use the current core version.

5. No Login Data

Because there is no login system in the current core version, PLATIVO does not request sign-in credentials.

6. Meal Names

You may enter meal names into PLATIVO. These names are stored locally on your device.

7. Meal Slots

PLATIVO may store whether a meal is assigned to Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner, or Snack.

8. Meal Notes

You may enter optional notes for meal ideas. These notes are stored locally on your device.

9. Favorite Meals

PLATIVO may store whether a meal is marked as a favorite.

10. Meal Rotation Count

PLATIVO may store how many times a meal has been planned so the app can show simple rotation statistics.

11. Weekly Meal Plan

PLATIVO may store the meals you assign to specific days of the week.

12. Planned Dates

The app may store the date associated with a planned meal.

13. Meal Plan Notes

You may save optional notes for a specific planned meal entry.

14. Meal History

PLATIVO may keep a local history of planned meals so you can review what you have scheduled before.

15. Copy Previous Week

If you use the copy-week feature, PLATIVO duplicates meal plan entries locally on your device.

16. Meal Templates

Reusable meal templates are stored locally for your convenience.

17. Shopping Notes

PLATIVO may store shopping-list or shopping-note text that you enter.

18. Shopping Checklist Status

The app may store whether a shopping note has been marked complete.

19. Search

Search terms used to filter meal ideas are processed locally on your device.

20. Filters

Meal-slot and other filters are applied locally and are not sent to a PLATIVO-operated server.

21. Statistics

PLATIVO may calculate local statistics such as total planned meals, favorite meals, most repeated meals, and rotation counts.

22. Most Repeated Meals

Most-repeated-meal statistics are calculated locally from your meal-planning history.

23. Dark Mode Preference

Your dark-mode preference may be stored locally so PLATIVO can remember your appearance choice.

24. Local Storage

The current core version uses local application storage, such as SharedPreferences, to store meal data, weekly plans, shopping notes, statistics, and preferences.

25. No Firebase

The current core version is not designed to use Firebase for authentication, databases, analytics, remote configuration, or cloud synchronization.

26. No Remote Backend

The current core version does not send your meal plans or shopping notes to a PLATIVO-operated backend.

27. No Cloud Sync

The current core version does not provide PLATIVO-operated cloud synchronization between devices.

28. No Advertising

The current core version does not contain PLATIVO-operated advertising.

29. No Personalized Ads

30. No Behavioral Analytics
